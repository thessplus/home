---
title: The One Where There Are Two Bottoms
date: 2026-11-06
category: Profits
description: What if your business is making money but losing its meaning? When
  profit is no longer enough to keep you going, what will?
---
Making money is a good reason to start a business, but is it enough to keep you going? What if a business has two bottom lines: one for profit and another for purpose? Purpose doesn't have to mean saving the world or making a difference on a grand scale. It can be as simple as providing good jobs, solving a problem, making someone's life easier, or building something you're proud of. Whatever it is, purpose should be woven into every part of the business, giving meaning to what it does and how it operates.

There's nothing wrong with wanting more profit; after all, even the best intentions need money to survive. But there may come a time when hitting another financial target no longer feels as rewarding as it used to. Jeff Fromm's *Purpose-Profit Loop* explores how purpose and profit can reinforce each other, with purpose helping drive business performance and profit helping sustain that purpose. Perhaps that's when we need to remind ourselves why we're doing what we're doing in the first place. **The Plus Side? When the numbers turn ugly, purpose gives you something worth fighting for.**
